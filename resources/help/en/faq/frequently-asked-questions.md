# Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How do I issue a ticket for a walk-in customer?
Click on **Queue** in the left navigation menu, then click **+ Issue Ticket**. Select the desired service, enter customer phone or name, and click **Print / Confirm**.

### 2. What is the difference between Queue and Appointments?
- **Queue**: For walk-in customers arriving on the spot. Tickets are served in sequential order.
- **Appointments**: For scheduled bookings made in advance for specific date/time slots.

### 3. When should I use Simple Queue mode?
Use **Simple Queue Mode** if you run a single counter, kiosk, or small reception where staff only need 1-click **Call Next** and **Complete** buttons.

### 4. What happens if a customer does not show up when called?
Click **Pass** or **Put on Hold**. If the customer returns later, you can click **Resume** to place them back into the active queue.

### 5. Can I reopen a cancelled ticket?
Yes! In **Advanced Queue Mode** or **Ticket History**, locate the cancelled ticket and click **Reopen Ticket**.

### 6. How do customers track their live position?
Customers can scan the **QR Code** printed on their ticket, open the link sent via **WhatsApp**, or check the **Public TV Display** screen in your waiting area.

### 7. How do I switch language or enable Dark Mode?
Click the **Globe icon** on the top navigation bar to select English, French, or Arabic. Click the **Moon / Sun icon** to toggle Dark Mode.
