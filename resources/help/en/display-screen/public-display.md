# Public TV Displays & Screen Pairing

Display live calling tickets on TVs, waiting room monitors, or tablet displays with visual and audio chime alerts.

---

## How to Set Up a TV Display

1. Navigate to **Configuration > Displays**.
2. Click **+ Add Display** or launch the public URL `/queue/display/{room-slug}`.
3. Press **F11** on your web browser or Smart TV browser to enter **Fullscreen Mode**.
4. Enable **Audio Chime** in display settings so tickets sound aloud when called (e.g. *"Ticket A-12 to Room 3"*).
