# Analytics & Operational Reports

Analyze real-time performance indicators to improve visitor experience and optimize staff distribution.

---

## Key Metrics Explained

- **Average Wait Time**: Total minutes customers spent waiting before being called.
- **Average Service Time**: Time taken at the counter to serve each customer.
- **Peak Hours Chart**: Visual breakdown of busiest arrival hours.
- **Staff Performance**: Breakdown of tickets completed by each team member.
