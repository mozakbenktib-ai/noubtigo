# Customer Live Tracking Portal

Customers do not need to sit in crowded waiting areas. They can track their live position from their smartphone.

---

## Tracking Channels

1. **Printed QR Code**: Scanning ticket QR code opens the live web tracker `/track/{token}`.
2. **WhatsApp Links**: Direct instant link sent to customer phone upon ticket creation.
3. **Customer Portal**: Customers logged into `/customer/dashboard` can save favorite companies and view active tickets.
