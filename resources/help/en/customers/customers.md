# Customer Management & History

Maintain a central repository of customer profiles, contact numbers, notes, and historical visit logs.

---

## Key Features

- **Search Customers**: Quickly search by full name, phone number, or national ID.
- **Customer History**: View all previous queue tickets, appointments, service types, and wait times.
- **WhatsApp Direct**: Trigger instant direct updates or ticket links directly to the customer's phone.
- **Archive Customer**: Safely soft-delete inactive or duplicate customer profiles.
