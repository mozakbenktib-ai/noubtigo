# Getting Started with Noubtigo

Welcome to **Noubtigo**, the modern Queue and Appointment Management Platform. Follow this step-by-step onboarding guide to set up your business workspace.

---

## Step-by-step Setup Workflow

```
[1. Company Setup] -> [2. Create Services] -> [3. Create Rooms] -> [4. Add Staff] -> [5. Issue Ticket] -> [6. Call Customer] -> [7. Serve & Complete]
```

### 1. Configure Your Company Settings
Go to **Settings > Company** to enter your business name, address, contact details, opening hours, and branding logo.

> [!TIP]
> Setting up your operating hours ensures accurate estimated wait times for customers tracking their turn.

---

### 2. Create Your Services
Services represent the tasks or consultations your business offers (e.g., *General Consultation*, *Cashier & Payment*, *Vehicle Inspection*).
- Go to **Services** in the sidebar menu.
- Click **+ New Service**.
- Define service name, prefix (e.g. **A** for Consultation), color badge, and estimated duration.

---

### 3. Create Service Rooms / Counters
Rooms or Counters are the exact physical places where services are delivered (e.g., *Doctor Office 1*, *Desk 3*, *Lane 2*).
- Go to **Rooms** in the sidebar.
- Click **+ Add Room**.
- Link one or multiple Services to this room.

---

### 4. Invite & Add Staff Members
Add your team (receptionists, doctors, service agents) and assign their operating rooms and services.
- Navigate to **Staff / Users** under Administration.
- Click **+ Add Staff Member**.
- Assign appropriate Role (**Secretary**, **Staff**, or **Company Admin**).

---

### 5. Issue Your First Ticket
Now you are ready to receive customers!
- Open **Queue Management**.
- Click **+ Issue Ticket**.
- Select the desired Service, enter customer phone or name, and click **Print / Issue Ticket**.

---

### 6. Call & Serve Customers
- On the **Queue Dashboard**, click **Call Next** to invoke the next waiting ticket.
- The Public Display TV and customer smartphone will announce the turn.
- Click **Start Serving** when the customer arrives at your desk.
- Once finished, click **Mark as Completed** or pass to another room if required.
