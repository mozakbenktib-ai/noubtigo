# Staff & Permission Management

Control staff accounts, permissions, and counter assignments.

---

## Roles Breakdown

- **Company Admin**: Full access to all settings, billing, reports, staff management, and queues.
- **Secretary / Receptionist**: Manages customer registration, issuing tickets, appointments, and queue dispatching.
- **Staff / Service Agent**: Access to Queue calling, serving customers, putting tickets on hold, and completing tickets.

## How to Add Staff
1. Go to **Administration > Staff**.
2. Click **+ Add Staff Member**.
3. Fill in name, email, default password, role, and assigned rooms.
