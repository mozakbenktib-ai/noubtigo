# Rooms & Counters Setup

Rooms represent physical desks, counters, or offices where services are provided.

---

## Examples of Rooms
- **Doctor Office 101**
- **Reception Desk 1**
- **Cashier Window 3**

## Managing Rooms
1. Go to **Rooms** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Room**.
3. Select which **Services** can be called in this room.
4. Assign default staff members to the room.
