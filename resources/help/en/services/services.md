# Services Configuration

Services define what operations your organization offers to visitors.

---

## What is a Service?
Examples include:
- **Medical Consultation** (Prefix: `A`, Duration: 15 mins)
- **Bill Payment & Cashier** (Prefix: `B`, Duration: 5 mins)
- **Document Registration** (Prefix: `C`, Duration: 10 mins)

## How to Setup Services
1. Go to **Services** under Configuration.
2. Click **+ Add Service**.
3. Choose a distinct letter prefix, color badge, and assign default serving duration.
4. Select which Rooms are allowed to serve this service.
