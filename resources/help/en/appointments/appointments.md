# Appointments Management Guide

Noubtigo allows businesses to accept advance bookings, manage time slots, and seamlessly integrate booked appointments into the daily queue.

---

## Appointment Lifecycle Workflow

```
[Booked / Scheduled] -> [Customer Arrival] -> [Check In] -> [Waiting Queue] -> [Serving] -> [Completed]
```

### Steps to Manage Appointments

1. **Creating an Appointment**:
   - Go to **Appointments** and click **+ New Appointment**.
   - Select Customer, Service, Date, and available Time Slot.
2. **Checking In Arriving Customers**:
   - When a scheduled customer arrives, click **Check In**.
   - The system automatically issues a queue ticket linked to their appointment.
3. **Slot Settings**:
   - Admins can configure working days, slot intervals (e.g. 15 mins), and capacity under **Configuration > Slot Settings**.
