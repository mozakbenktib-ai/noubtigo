# Company & System Settings

Customise your company workspace parameters.

---

## Settings Sections

- **Company Profile**: Business name, email, phone, and address.
- **Queue Mode**: Toggle between **Simple Queue** and **Advanced Queue**.
- **Branding**: Primary theme colors, logo, and public display banner text.
